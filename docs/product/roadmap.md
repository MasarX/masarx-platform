# Product roadmap

The roadmap describes the intended sequence of capabilities.

1. Establish tenant access, the shipment data model, configurable webhook intake and durable audit records.
2. Connect shipment validation, address resolution, label generation and customer notifications through shared workflow templates.
3. Add operator workspaces for exception review, dispatch, reporting and integration troubleshooting.
4. Extend field execution with pickup, route assignment, proof of delivery and reconciliation.

Delivery depends on verification of the underlying contracts. The [testing playbook](../tests/PLAYBOOK.md) describes the scenarios used for that verification.
