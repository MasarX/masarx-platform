# Product mission

MasarX helps delivery teams manage shipments and coordinate integrations, warehouse activity, field execution and customer communication.

Operators need to see the current shipment state, the decisions that produced it and the actions available to their role. Integration operators need configurable input mapping, observable workflows and predictable retries. Customers need scoped shipment status and clear delivery options.

Use shared workflow and policy definitions across applications. Resolve policy from the applicable scopes and record the winning definition with the operational decision. Keep tenant data isolated and grant each role only the access it needs.
